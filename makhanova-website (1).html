WHERE IT COMES FROM

A crop that only grows where the water is right.

Phool Makhana comes from the seed of Euryale ferox, an aquatic plant that thrives in the shallow ponds of Mithila — the Darbhanga, Madhubani, Purnia and Katihar belt of Bihar, which together grow the majority of the world's makhana, and which gave India the Madhubani painting tradition this brand's visual identity draws from.

New Foxnourish Pvt. Ltd. works directly with pond-owning farmers and harvester cooperatives across this belt. We take the raw seed after monsoon harvest and manage grading, roasting and export in-house — so every lot can be traced back to the ponds it came from.

"Buyers don't just want a snack ingredient. They want to know who grew it, and whether the next container will match the sample." SOURCING PHILOSOPHY, NEW FOXNOURISH PVT. LTD.
FROM POND TO PORT

Five steps, one lot at a time.

Every export batch moves through the same controlled sequence, so the container that arrives matches the sample you approved.

Hand harvest

Seeds gathered from pond beds by trained harvesters through the monsoon season.

Sort & grade

Cleaned and separated by size and density before roasting begins.

Roast & pop

Pan-roasted at controlled temperature until the seed pops into the familiar puff.

Test & pack

Moisture, aflatoxin and microbial checks, then nitrogen-flushed packing.

Ship from Kolkata

Every export consignment departs via Kolkata Port, with documentation handled in-house.
