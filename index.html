WHERE IT COMES FROM

A crop that only grows where the water is right.

Phool Makhana comes from the seed of Euryale ferox, an aquatic plant that thrives in the shallow ponds of Mithila — the Darbhanga, Madhubani, Purnia and Katihar belt of Bihar, which together grow the majority of the world's makhana, and which gave India the Madhubani painting tradition this brand's visual identity draws from.

New Fox Nourish works directly with pond-owning farmers and harvester cooperatives across this belt. We take the raw seed after monsoon harvest and manage grading, roasting and export in-house — under two brands built for two different buyers: MakhanaG for importers and manufacturers buying in bulk, and Makhanova for retailers who want a shelf-ready roasted pack.

"Buyers don't just want a snack ingredient. They want to know who grew it, and whether the next container will match the sample." SOURCING PHILOSOPHY, NEW FOX NOURISH
INSIDE THE SUPPLY CHAIN

What happens between the pond and the pallet.

A quick look at harvesting and processing before the seed ever reaches grading.

Hand harvesting

Trained harvesters pole small boats across the pond and lift ripe pods by hand through the monsoon season.

Sun drying

Harvested seed is spread on mats to dry naturally before it moves to roasting — the step that fixes moisture content.

Roasting & popping

Pan-roasted over controlled heat until each seed pops — the origin of the puffed makhana buyers recognise.

Grading by Suta

Popped seed is sorted into 4, 5, 6+ and 7+ Suta piles by size before it's routed to the right brand and buyer.

GRADED BY SUTA

Graded for the buyer, not just the crop.

We export four Suta grades — the traditional Mithila trade measure of popped makhana size. The higher the Suta, the bigger and more uniform the puff.

What is "Suta"? Suta is how the Bihar makhana trade has always sized popped fox nuts — larger Suta numbers mean bigger, rounder, more uniform puffs, and command a higher price. We lab-test and hand-check every lot against these four Suta grades before it's approved for export.
4 Suta MakhanaG
